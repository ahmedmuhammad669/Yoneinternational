# SEO and content checklist

- Set the final HTTPS origin in `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
- Verify canonical host, redirects, `robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml`.
- Confirm unique title and description for each published category, product, article, album and job.
- Validate Organization, BreadcrumbList, Product, Article and JobPosting JSON-LD against visible verified fields.
- Never add Review/AggregateRating, Offer, price, stock or shipping schema without maintained public facts.
- Confirm draft, admin, private media and private enquiry routes are not indexable.
- Check descriptive filenames, dimensions, captions and alt text.
- Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Scan broken links and missing images after every bulk import.
- Test one URL change and verify the managed 301 redirect.
- Avoid thin location pages, unsupported claims and keyword stuffing.
